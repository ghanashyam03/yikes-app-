# yikes! — Specification Glossary

All definitions below are quoted and compiled verbatim from [`SPEC_FULL.md`](./SPEC_FULL.md).

---

### Playbook
> "Personal Playbook. A short quiz at the start builds a profile that reshapes the whole app." (Section 1)
>
> "The quiz takes about three minutes, one question per screen, and ends with a real warm-up chat and a Playbook the user can read and edit. Every answer changes something visible in the app, and the table below says what. ... It keeps learning. After two weeks the app proposes changes from behaviour ('Level 2 looks easy now. Try something tougher?'), and any answer can be re-taken from You, then Playbook." (Section 4)
>
> "The Playbook assembles as glass tiles, each answering a question: Your pace (thinks first, speaks well), Your battery (how fast it drains and what recharges it), Your hardest places, Your first 3 steps, Your Coach (tone)." (Section 9, Screen A8)

---

### Moment
> "Moments. Real events you add (a meeting, a party). The app preps you before and debriefs you after." (Section 1)
>
> "A Moment is a real upcoming event: a meeting, a party, a call, a networking night. Users add one by hand in 15 seconds (what, who, when, how formal), or pick events from their calendar if they choose to connect it (read-only, only the events they tick). Each Moment has a three-step life:
> - **Before**: a notification the evening before and an hour before, with a Prep card and a 2-minute rehearsal.
> - **During**: one tap opens a quiet pocket card with the opener, two questions and an exit line.
> - **After**: a nudge 30 minutes later for the energy rating and a short Debrief chat." (Section 7)

---

### Session
> "A session takes 3 to 5 minutes and has six beats. It can be paused and resumed, and every beat can be skipped. Idea (20 seconds)... Watch (40 seconds)... Read (about 60 seconds)... Try (about 90 seconds)... Coach (30 seconds)... Out there (optional)." (Section 6)
>
> "The promise. In about four minutes: learn one move, rehearse it with an AI character, get coaching that quotes your own words, and pick one small step for a real conversation. Over weeks, a personal journal shows what changed." (Section 1)

---

### Inner Voice
> "Inner Voice. After every chat the user can read what the partner was privately thinking at each turn ('Okay, a real question. I like that.'). The partner writes its thought with every reply, so the reveal is genuine to the scene." (Section 5)
>
> "The signature screen. The chat replays slowly in a dim view; after each partner line, a dashed thought bubble drifts up beside her portrait, tinted in her colour. The user scrubs a timeline to jump between turns and taps any thought to open the matching Coach note. A Done button returns to the Coach." (Section 10, Screen B9)
>
> "Characters are simulated. Inner Voice shows what the character was written to think. The app never says it reveals what real people think, and the Read step always includes Different people, different signals." (Section 14)

---

### Coach
> "Coach. Feedback in plain language that quotes your lines and rewrites them in your own voice." (Section 1)
>
> "Coach. Feedback with three parts: moments that worked (quoting the user's lines and why), one pivot moment (what happened and an alternative in the user's own voice), and one thing to try next. Every quote is checked by code to exist in the transcript." (Section 5)
>
> "The feedback screen, in three stacked glass tiles with a Coach avatar: What worked (Mint rule): two or three of the user's own lines in quote bubbles, each with a one-line reason. A pivot moment (Honey rule): the turn where the chat changed, what happened, and a rewrite in the user's own voice with a Try it button. Try this next time (Lilac rule): one concrete idea." (Section 10, Screen B8)

---

### Gap
> "The Gap Game. Before a real step, the user forecasts how draining it will be (0 to 10) and how warm the other person will be. Afterwards they record what happened. The Gap is the difference. Over weeks the user watches their forecasts move closer to reality, which is the app's quiet proof that things go better than feared." (Section 7)
>
> "A line chart of forecast against reality over weeks, with the gap shaded. A caption in plain words (Your forecasts are getting closer to what actually happens). Tapping a point opens that check-in." (Section 11, Screen C16)

---

### Quiet Ladder
> "The Quiet Ladder. A personal ladder of 8 to 10 real steps from easy to hard ('say hi to a neighbour' up to 'ask a question in a meeting'), seeded by the quiz and editable. Climbing it is the progression. Every rung is the user's choice." (Section 7)
>
> "A vertical ladder of 8 to 10 rungs with current rung highlighted. Tapping a rung shows what it is, a suggested micro-rep, and Mark done. A Reorder and Add rung option; the user can rewrite any rung in their own words. A small AI button suggests the next rung." (Section 11, Screen C18)

---

### Battery (Social Battery)
> "Social Battery. Track what drains and recharges you, so practice respects your energy." (Section 1)
>
> "Social Battery. A one-tap rating of energy before and after any Moment or practice. Over time the app shows what drains and what recharges ('Large groups after 6pm cost you most; one-to-ones at lunch recharge you') and suggests recovery: 'Thursday is heavy. Keep Friday evening free.' It is information, never a score to maximise." (Section 7)
>
> "A single large battery that the user drags up or down, with soft haptics at each step. Optional tag chips (people, noise, length, who). Button Save. Takes under 10 seconds. Shown before and after a Moment." (Section 11, Screen C6)

---

### Rhythm
> "Rhythm, not streaks. A weekly ring fills toward the user's own goal (2 to 5 sessions). Missing a week changes nothing. A daily streak with freezes is available only if the user switches it on in Settings." (Section 7)
>
> "Greeting row: a serif greeting by the user's name and time of day, a small battery icon showing today's energy, and a weekly rhythm ring." (Section 10, Screen B1)

---

### Partner (Practice Partner)
> "Practice Partner. A live chat with a character who has a persona card, a scenario and a hidden mood. Characters are realistic, not agreeable: reserved at first, not oversharing, sometimes distracted, able to wrap up the chat. They never claim to be human." (Section 5)

---

### Persona
> "A live chat with a character who has a persona card, a scenario and a hidden mood." (Section 5)
>
> "The gateway builds the prompt: persona card, scenario, Playbook summary (not raw history), recent turns, hidden mood." (Section 5)
>
> "Characters are simulated. Inner Voice shows what the character was written to think. The app never says it reveals what real people think... The app does not claim a real person liked, disliked or understood the user." (Section 14)

---

### Wince
> "Tone. A clever, kind friend. The name is the wince after an awkward moment; the app meets it with warmth and a dry joke. Never clinical, never preachy." (Section 1)
>
> "Mascot 'Wince': a small squishy blob with a tiny squint. It lives in empty states, loading and the launch screen with 6 poses (curious, sleepy, thinking, proud, peeking shyly, offline). It never speaks and never acts as a friend or companion." (Section 8)
