# Crisis Resources Directory

> [!IMPORTANT]

> All telephone and text helpline details below are verified through official government public health bodies and certified non-profit registries.

## India (Launch Market - Primary Locale)

- **Tele-MANAS (Government of India, National Tele Mental Health Programme)**
  - **Phone**: 14416 (Toll-Free, 24x7, Multilingual across all Indian languages)
  - **Alternate Phone**: 1800-891-4416
  - **Website**: https://telemanas.mohfw.gov.in
  - **Verification**: Verified official National Mental Health initiative under Ministry of Health and Family Welfare (MoHFW), Govt of India.

- **Vandrevala Foundation Helpline**
  - **Phone / WhatsApp**: +91 9999 666 555 (24x7, Free, Confidential)
  - **Website**: https://www.vandrevalafoundation.com
  - **Verification**: Verified non-profit mental health counselling service operating nationally across India.

- **KIRAN Mental Health Rehabilitation Helpline**
  - **Phone**: 1800-599-0019 (24x7, Toll-Free)
  - **Website**: Department of Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities, Govt of India.
  - **Verification**: Verified official helpline launched by Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment.

## United States

- **988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline**
  - **Phone & Text**: 988 (24x7, Free, Confidential, English & Spanish)
  - **Chat**: https://988lifeline.org
  - **Verification**: Verified official federal crisis number administered by SAMHSA.

- **Crisis Text Line**
  - **SMS**: Text HOME to 741741 (24x7, Free)
  - **Website**: https://www.crisistextline.org
  - **Verification**: Verified 501(c)(3) non-profit organization.

  - **Verification**: Registered UK charity (1089464) and crisis support network.

- **SHOUT Crisis Text Service**
