# Store Listing Copy

## App Name
yikes! — Tough conversations, practiced safely.

## Subtitle
Rehearse what to say before it matters.

## Description
yikes! helps you prepare for tricky conversations with friends, family, and colleagues. Practice out loud with an AI partner, explore safe guidance from a supportive coach, and build confidence at your own pace.

Features:
- Realistic rehearsal partner with customizable tones.
- Actionable debriefs and warm reflection prompts.
- Complete privacy: your data belongs to you, with one-tap export and deletion.

Note: yikes! is a communication practice tool and does not provide clinical therapy, medical diagnosis, or professional mental healthcare.
