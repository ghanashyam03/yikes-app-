=== T-04 TabBar Accessibility and Touch Targets Audit ===
Specification requirements from docs/spec/04-design-system.md & TASKS.md T-04:
1. 48pt minimum touch targets on all interactive elements
2. accessibilityRole="tab" for tabs, accessibilityRole="button" for center practice action
3. VoiceOver & TalkBack accessibilityLabel on all tabs and practice button
4. High contrast across Daylight and Midnight modes
5. Liquid blob Reanimated worklet respecting Reduced Motion
6. Icon press bounce with Reanimated worklet

- TabItemButton accessibilityState: { selected: isFocused }. PASS.

- PracticeCenterButton accessibilityRole: accessibilityRole="button". PASS.
- PracticeCenterButton accessibilityLabel: navigationCopy.practiceButton.accessibilityLabel. PASS.
